MQ-2 Gas Sensor & Buzzer Hardware Setup

1. Wiring Connections:

  • Sensor VCC: Connect to 5V (heater needs 5V).
  • Sensor GND: Connect to ESP8266 GND.
  • Sensor AOUT: Connect to ESP8266 A0 pin.
  • Active Buzzer: Connect positive to Pin D1 (GPIO5) and negative to GND.
  • Solenoid Relay: Connect to Pin D2 (GPIO4) Active Low.

2. Firmware Upload:

  1. Open firmware/gas_leakage_detector.ino in Arduino IDE.
  2. Set your Wi-Fi credentials in the sketch.
  3. Verify buzzer & relay pin configurations.
  4. Upload to NodeMCU 1.0 at 115200 baud.
