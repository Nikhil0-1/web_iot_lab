# Project 07: TDS Water Quality Monitor

## 1. Project Overview
The **TDS Water Quality Monitor** quantifies Total Dissolved Solids in parts per million (PPM) by analyzing electrical conductivity across immersion probe electrodes. Features an animated water beaker, purity dial gauge, and classification into *Pure RO*, *Clean Tap*, *Hard Mineral*, and *Non-potable*.

## 2. Features
- **Purity Meter Dial Gauge:** Smooth animated needle rotation ranging from 0 to 1000 PPM.
- **Water Grade Categorization:** Direct feedback on mineral content based on WHO guidelines.
- **Probe Dry Detection:** Automatic fault warning if probe is exposed to open air.
- **CSV Data Export:** Track water purification filters over time.

## 3. Important Safety & Scientific Disclaimer
> [!WARNING]
> TDS measures **dissolved conductive ions and minerals** (calcium, magnesium, chloride, sodium). Low TDS **does NOT** guarantee water is biologically safe or free of harmful viruses, bacteria, protozoa, or organic toxins (such as pesticides or microplastics).
