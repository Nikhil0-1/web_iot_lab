Hardware Setup — Project 27: Door Access Controller

Components: NodeMCU ESP8266, 12V Solenoid Bolt, 1-Channel Optoisolated Relay Module, 1N4007 Diode, Buzzer.

Connections:

  • Relay IN → D1 (GPIO 5)
  • Status LED → D2 (GPIO 4)
  • Buzzer Alert → D8 (GPIO 15)
  • Solenoid Power → External 12V 1A DC adapter
  • Protection: Connect 1N4007 diode in reverse across solenoid coil terminals to suppress inductive flyback voltage spikes.

Firmware: Flash firmware/door_access.ino.
