# Project 31 Wiring Guide — Visitor Counter

## Dual Break-Beam Pinouts
- **Sensor A Receiver OUT**: NodeMCU D1 (GPIO 5) [Internal pull-up enabled]
- **Sensor B Receiver OUT**: NodeMCU D2 (GPIO 4) [Internal pull-up enabled]
- **Sensors VCC & GND**: 5V External Power & Common GND
- **Transit Pulse LED**: NodeMCU D6 (GPIO 12) via 220Ω resistor.
- **Physical Mounting**: Mount Beam A and Beam B parallel across doorway, spaced roughly 15cm–25cm apart at chest height (1.1m from floor).
