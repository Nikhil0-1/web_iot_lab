INA219 Voltage Rating Notice

The Texas Instruments INA219 supports a maximum bus voltage of 26V DC and ±3.2A with the standard 0.1Ω shunt resistor. Do not connect larger solar strings (>24V open-circuit Voc) directly to the INA219 breakout.

Hardware Setup — Project 34: Solar Panel Monitor

Components: NodeMCU ESP8266, INA219 I²C High-Side Current & Voltage Sensor, 12V/18V Photovoltaic Panel.

I²C Bus Pinout:

  • INA219 VCC → 3.3V NodeMCU
  • INA219 GND → Common GND
  • INA219 SCL → NodeMCU D1 (GPIO 5)
  • INA219 SDA → NodeMCU D2 (GPIO 4)
  • VIN+ → Solar Panel Positive (+)
  • VIN- → Battery Charge Controller (+) / Load (+)

Firmware: Flash firmware/solar_monitor.ino using Adafruit_INA219 library.
