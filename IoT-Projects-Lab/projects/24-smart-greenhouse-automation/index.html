Setup — Project 24: Smart Greenhouse Automation

Components: NodeMCU ESP8266, DHT11/22 Temperature & Humidity Sensor, Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor, LDR Light Sensor, Dual 5V Relay Board (Fan & Pump), Float Switch (Dry-run safety), External 12V / 5V Supplies.

GPIO Map:

  • DHT DATA → D4 (GPIO 2) with 10k pull-up to 3.3V
  • Capacitive Soil Sensor → A0 (ADC 0–1V / 3.3V)
  • Exhaust Fan Relay → D1 (GPIO 5) — Active LOW
  • Irrigation Pump Relay → D2 (GPIO 4) — Active LOW
  • Water Reservoir Float Switch → D5 (GPIO 14) with internal pull-up

Firmware: Flash firmware/smart_greenhouse.ino via Arduino IDE.

Interlock Protection: Simulated and hardware irrigation pumps immediately shut down if the water reservoir is reported empty, preventing pump burn-out.
