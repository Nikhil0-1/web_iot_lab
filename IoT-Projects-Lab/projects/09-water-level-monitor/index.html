HC-SR04 & ESP8266 Hardware Setup

1. Wiring Connections:

  • VCC: Connect to 5V external power (HC-SR04 ultrasonic transducer requires 5V).
  • GND: Connect to common ground with ESP8266.
  • Trigger: Connect to Pin D5 (GPIO14) on NodeMCU.
  • Echo: Connect through a 1kΩ / 2kΩ voltage divider to Pin D6 (GPIO12) to safely step down the 5V echo pulse to 3.3V for ESP8266 input protection.

2. Firmware Upload:

  1. Open firmware/water_level_monitor.ino in Arduino IDE.
  2. Configure your Wi-Fi SSID and Password in the sketch.
  3. Configure tank height parameter (default: 100 cm).
  4. Upload to ESP8266 at 115200 baud.
