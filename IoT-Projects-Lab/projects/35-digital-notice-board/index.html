Hardware Setup — Project 35: Digital Notice Board

Components: NodeMCU ESP8266, 0.96" SSD1306 128×64 I²C OLED Display, Passive Piezo Buzzer.

I²C Bus Pinout:

  • OLED VCC → 3.3V NodeMCU
  • OLED GND → Common GND
  • OLED SCL → NodeMCU D1 (GPIO 5)
  • OLED SDA → NodeMCU D2 (GPIO 4)
  • Chime Buzzer → NodeMCU D5 (GPIO 14) via 2N2222 transistor

Firmware: Flash firmware/digital_notice.ino with Adafruit_SSD1306 & Adafruit_GFX.
