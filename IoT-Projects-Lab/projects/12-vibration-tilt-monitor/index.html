MPU-6050 6-Axis IMU Hardware Setup

1. Wiring Connections:

  • VCC: Connect to 3.3V on NodeMCU.
  • GND: Connect to ESP8266 GND.
  • SCL: Connect to ESP8266 Pin D1 (GPIO5).
  • SDA: Connect to ESP8266 Pin D2 (GPIO4).
  • AD0: Connect to GND for I2C Address 0x68.

2. Firmware Configuration:

  1. Open firmware/vibration_tilt_monitor.ino in Arduino IDE.
  2. Include Wire.h and MPU6050_light.h.
  3. Flash to ESP8266 NodeMCU at 115200 baud.
