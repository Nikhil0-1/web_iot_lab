ADC Voltage Protection Notice

NodeMCU A0 input is rated for 3.3V maximum. Always choose precision 1% metal-film resistors with ratio $R_2 / (R_1 + R_2)$ sized so that maximum charging voltage never exceeds 3.0V at the pin.

Hardware Setup — Project 33: Battery Monitor

Components: NodeMCU ESP8266, 100kΩ and 10kΩ 1% Precision Metal Film Resistors, 100nF Ceramic Capacitor.

Voltage Divider Schematic:

  • Battery (+) → 100kΩ Resistor → NodeMCU A0 Pin
  • NodeMCU A0 Pin → 10kΩ Resistor → Battery (-) Common GND
  • A0 to GND: Connect 100nF capacitor in parallel with 10kΩ to filter high-frequency ADC noise.
  • Divider Ratio: 10k / (100k + 10k) = 1/11 (Max safe voltage: 3.3V × 11 = 36.3V).

Firmware: Flash firmware/battery_monitor.ino.
