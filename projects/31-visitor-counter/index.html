Hardware Setup — Project 31: Visitor Counter

Components: NodeMCU ESP8266, Dual IR Break-Beam Transmitters & Receivers (Sensors A & B), 10kΩ Pull-ups.

Wiring:

  • Sensor A Receiver Out → NodeMCU D1 (GPIO 5)
  • Sensor B Receiver Out → NodeMCU D2 (GPIO 4)
  • Transmitters → 5V External & GND
  • Indicator LED → NodeMCU D6 (GPIO 12) via 220Ω resistor

Firmware: Flash firmware/visitor_counter.ino.
