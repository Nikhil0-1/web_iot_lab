CdS Photoresistor Hardware Setup

1. Voltage Divider Circuit:

  • Connect one leg of the LDR to 3.3V.
  • Connect other leg of LDR to ESP8266 A0 (Analog Input).
  • Connect a 10kΩ pull-down resistor between A0 and GND.

2. Firmware Configuration:

  1. Open firmware/ldr_light_intensity_monitor.ino in Arduino IDE.
  2. Set your Wi-Fi SSID and Password.
  3. Flash to ESP8266 NodeMCU at 115200 baud.
