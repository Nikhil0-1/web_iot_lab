YF-S201 Water Flow Sensor Hardware Setup

1. Wiring Connections:

  • Red Wire (VCC): Connect to 5V (or 3.3V depending on sensor module).
  • Black Wire (GND): Connect to ESP8266 GND.
  • Yellow Wire (Pulse Out): Connect to ESP8266 Pin D2 (GPIO4) with a 10kΩ pull-up to 3.3V.

2. Firmware Configuration:

  1. Open firmware/water_flow_meter.ino in Arduino IDE.
  2. Ensure FALLING or RISING edge interrupt is attached to D2.
  3. Upload to NodeMCU 1.0 at 115200 baud.
