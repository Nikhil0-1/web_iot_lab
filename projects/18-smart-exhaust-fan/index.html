Setup — Project 18: Smart Exhaust Fan

Components: NodeMCU ESP8266, DHT11 or DHT22 Temperature & Humidity Sensor, 5V Opto-isolated Relay module, 10kΩ pull-up resistor.

Wiring:

  • DHT DATA → D4 (GPIO 2) with 10k pull-up to 3.3V
  • Relay IN → D1 (GPIO 5) — Active LOW
  • Relay VCC → 5V (VIN), Relay GND → GND
  • Fan power routed through Relay COM & NO contacts

Firmware: Flash firmware/smart_exhaust_fan.ino via Arduino IDE.

Hysteresis: Fan automatically activates when Temperature > threshold OR Humidity > threshold. Shuts off only when both parameters drop 2.0°C and 5% RH below thresholds to prevent rapid relay cycling.
