# Project 32 · Smart Energy Meter

## Overview
A precision true RMS AC electrical monitoring dashboard powered by the PZEM-004T v3.0 transducer with current transformer (CT) clamp, power factor analysis, and configurable billing tariff calculation.

## Features
- Real-time active power waveform canvas trend line.
- Comprehensive electrical telemetry: Voltage (V), Current (A), Active Power (W), Energy (kWh), Frequency (Hz), and Power Factor.
- Dynamic electricity bill cost estimator based on user-defined tariff rates.
- Strict electrical safety warnings and galvanically isolated TTL communication.
