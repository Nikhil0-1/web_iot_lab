TDS Water Quality Sensor Hardware Setup

1. Wiring Connections:

  • VCC: Connect to 3.3V or 5V (depending on adapter module).
  • GND: Connect to ESP8266 GND.
  • AOUT (Analog): Connect to ESP8266 A0 pin.

2. Firmware Configuration:

  1. Open firmware/tds_water_quality_monitor.ino in Arduino IDE.
  2. Set reference voltage and temperature coefficient in code.
  3. Upload to NodeMCU 1.0 at 115200 baud.
